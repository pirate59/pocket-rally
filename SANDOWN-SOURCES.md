# Sandown course reference

The game represents the current 3.1 km national circuit at Sandown International Motor Raceway, running anticlockwise. It does not use the former extended international circuit.

- [Supercars circuit profile](https://www.supercars.com/circuit/sandown-international-motor-raceway): direction, length, long straights and downhill esses.
- [Supercars 2025 event map](https://d35kvm5iuwjt9t.cloudfront.net/pdf/SANDOWN25_TrackMap.pdf): route, numbered corners, start/finish, pits, main grandstand, pedestrian bridge, lakes, paddock and railway/station placement. This map is explicitly indicative rather than to scale; the game is a stylised approximation.
- [Sandown Revival 2026 venue map](https://sandownrevival.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/J20677-Sandown-2026_External-Site-Map-v3.pdf): horse-racing courses, lake, grandstand and paddock geography.
- [Warren Luff's MOTOR track guide](https://www.whichcar.com.au/features/motor-track-guide-sandown-raceway): crest before Turn 6, descending esses toward Turn 9, paved runoff and corner sequence.

Elevation is an interpretation of the driver guide, not measured survey data. A continuous hill under the road and surrounding board models the back-straight climb and downhill esses. No precise real-world elevation figures are displayed. Architecture, vegetation and railways are simplified diorama scenery; event-specific sponsor branding is omitted.
