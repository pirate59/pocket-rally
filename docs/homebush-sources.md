# Sydney 500 · Homebush

Historic 2009–2016 Sydney Olympic Park street circuit: 3.420 km, 13 turns, anti-clockwise. This is the Homebush event, not the later Sydney Motorsport Park event with the same name.

Sources consulted 16 September 2026:
- Supercars, direction and temporary-circuit construction: https://www.supercars.com/news/sydney-circuit-taking-shape
- RacingCircuits.info, historic map and circuit history: https://www.racingcircuits.info/australasia/australia/sydney-olympic-park.html
- V8 Sleuth, photographic lap and street/landmark placement: https://www.v8sleuth.com.au/sydney-olympic-park-supercars-track-today/

Layout follows the historic map at miniature scale. Start/finish is on Australia Avenue; Kevin Coombs Avenue leads to the T2–4 chicane, Edwin Flack Avenue to the T6–7 crossover, and T8 opens onto Dawn Fraser Avenue. Showground Road and Murray Rose Avenue lead through the final Park Street chicane to T13. Tight chicanes are slightly opened for the game's cars; overall length is a real-world reference, not a surveyed game distance.

Elevation is interpretive, with gentle precinct relief and stronger continuous undulations along Dawn Fraser Avenue approaching T9. No reliable surveyed elevation profile was found. Exact height values are intentionally absent from the HUD, and the course selection labels elevation approximate. Reported adverse camber at T8 is research context only: the current game road model has no independent crossfall or camber physics. No artificial jumps are added.

Scenery represents race-era Olympic Park in the established miniature style: ANZ Stadium, Olympic arena (sponsor-neutral name), Showground Stadium, exhibition-hall paddock, Australia Avenue pits, Olympic Park station, Novotel, grandstands, light poles, fence posts and trees. Buildings are stylised, not architectural replicas. No source photography or map artwork is distributed in the game.

Playability revision: Olympic and final-sector chicanes have longer, gentler transitions, and the driving surface is wider. These deliberately approximate the historic layout for miniature cars. Floating landmark labels appear only in course overview; during racing, section names remain in the HUD.
