# Hidden Valley Raceway course reference

The game represents the permanent road-racing circuit at Hidden Valley Raceway,
Darwin, Northern Territory, running clockwise.

## Circuit facts used

- **2.870 km, 14 corners, clockwise, with a main straight of about 1.1 km.**
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Valley_Raceway),
  [Supercars circuit profile](https://www.supercars.com/circuit/hidden-valley-raceway),
  [Austadiums](https://www.austadiums.com/stadiums/hidden-valley-raceway))
- **Turn 1** is the tight, slow corner at the end of the long straight and the
  main overtaking point; drivers can take it around the outside and set up Turn 5.
  ([Craig's Corner: The challenges of Hidden Valley](https://www.supercars.com/news/craigs-corner-the-challenges-of-hidden-valley))
- **Turns 2, 3 and 4** are fast *uphill* esses taken left–right–left, with the
  highest speed of the lap reached just before the Turn 5 braking point.
  ([MCNews track guide](https://www.mcnews.com.au/hidden-valley-track-guide-map-lap/),
  [PitBoard track guide](https://pitboard.com.au/track-guide-hidden-valley-darwin-nt/))
- **Turn 5** is a right-hander and a heavy-braking passing place, with tyre walls
  arranged as a chicane on the exit to stop cars short-cutting the corner.
- **Turn 6** is the slowest corner of the lap (about 57 km/h at the apex), a
  hairpin sitting in a natural amphitheatre "almost like a stadium"; the Turn 5–6
  complex produces switchbacks and cutback passes.
- **Turn 7** is known locally as "Ducati"; its exit is blind until the apex, and
  Turns 7–8 run almost as one long double-apex before the **8–9 right–left kink**.
  ([PitBoard track guide](https://pitboard.com.au/track-guide-hidden-valley-darwin-nt/))
- **Turn 10** follows a long run and is one of the most daunting corners in
  Australia; **Turns 11, 12 and 13** are linked flicks, and **Turn 14** is the
  slow corner that decides the exit onto the start/finish straight.
- **Complex facilities**: a 1 km ANDRA drag strip alongside the main straight, the
  400 m Northline Speedway dirt oval, a kart circuit, a permanent motocross track
  built beside the Turn 6 hairpin, 30 pit garages and control towers, and
  **no grandstand** — spectators watch from grassed terraces.
  ([NT Government facility listing](https://nt.gov.au/leisure/sport/facilities-and-contacts/find-a-sports-facility/hidden-valley-motor-sports-complex),
  [Austadiums](https://www.austadiums.com/stadiums/hidden-valley-raceway),
  [Speedcafe on the MXGP track](https://speedcafe.com/motorsport-news-2025-mxgp-darwin-hidden-valley-raceway-track-revealed-details-photos/))

## What is authored rather than sourced

The centreline is a **plan authored for this game** from the facts and lap guides
above: no circuit map artwork is traced or bundled, and no survey data is used.
Corner order, handedness where the guides state it, relative corner speed and the
share of the lap taken by the main straight are reproduced; exact corner radii,
the site's true compass orientation and the precise positions of buildings are an
interpretation for miniature gameplay.

Elevation is likewise interpretive. A continuous rise under the road and the
surrounding board carries the climb through the esses, the drop into the Turn 6
bowl and the rise to the Turn 10 crest. No real-world elevation figures are
claimed, so the HUD shows section names without a height readout. Architecture,
the support tracks and the Top End woodland are simplified diorama scenery, and
event-specific sponsor branding is omitted.
