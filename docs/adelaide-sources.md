# Adelaide 500

Recreates the 3.219 km, 14-turn Adelaide 500 Supercars layout, not the longer former Formula 1 circuit or proposed 2027 replacement. The game scales distances for its miniature cars.

- Official 2024 event map, used for the centreline, turn numbers, pit position, grandstands, paddock, concert precinct, pedestrian bridges and park names: https://assets.adelaide500.com.au/app/uploads/2024/11/05075333/2325_ADL500_Track-Map-Final-V3.pdf
- Supercars circuit guide, clockwise direction and current street-circuit character: https://www.supercars.com/circuit/adelaide-parklands-circuit
- Supercars driving guide, 14 turns and racing character: https://www.supercars.com/news/hino-track-guide-adelaide
- Surface and permanent Victoria Park section: https://www.supercars.com/news/drivers-braced-for-tricky-adelaide-surface-turn-8-aero-affect
- Rymill Lake and event precinct reference: https://assets.adelaide500.com.au/app/uploads/2025/11/11170611/3377-BP-Grand-Final-Map-Update-%C6%92.pdf

The official event diagram is indicative, not surveyed. Terrain uses a subtle continuous parkland gradient across the board; no verified detailed elevation survey was found, so heights are interpretive and the game does not display exact metre values. Scenery is a stylised representation, not architectural reproduction. Existing smooth barriers, runoff penalties, physics, boost settings and cameras apply.
