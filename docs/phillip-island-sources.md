# Phillip Island motorcycle course

The layout follows the circuit operator's [official circuit map](https://www.phillipislandcircuit.com.au/circuit-info/circuit-map/) and its [2022 map PDF](https://www.phillipislandcircuit.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/2022-Circuit-Map-Colour-No-Gridlines.pdf). The race starts on Gardner Straight, proceeding toward Doohan Corner. All twelve turns are represented.

[MotoGP's rider guide](https://esport.motogp.com/index.php/news/jack-millers-guide-to-phillip-island) describes the flowing corners, undulations and Lukey Heights. The hill and descending approach to MG extend across the surrounding terrain. Heights are an artistic approximation, not surveyed elevation data.

Coastal scenery, paddock buildings, spectator stands and gravel runoff are informed by the operator map and the [Australian Motorcycle Grand Prix precinct map](https://www.motogp.com.au/uploads/images/2023-MotoGP-MxM-DEVICE.pdf). Scenery footprints are checked against the road before placement.

Motorcycles use a separate finite-rate lean model. Lean drives yaw, while acceleration and braking consume available tyre grip. Ground slope influences momentum; grass reduces grip and speed. Only the cockpit camera banks with the bike. External camera modes remain upright. This is approachable game handling, not a MotoGP engineering simulation.
